About TeamSpin Pro

TeamSpin Pro is a fair, transparent, and high-performance random decision tool designed for teachers, event organizers, and gamers worldwide. We prioritize speed and mobile-friendliness to ensure everyone can make quick decisions on the go.

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 2026

At TeamSpin Pro, we value your privacy. This policy explains how we handle information.

1. Data Collection

We do not store the names you enter into the picker wheel on our servers. All processing happens locally on your device's browser.

We use Google AdSense to serve ads. Google may use cookies (like the DART cookie) to serve ads based on your visit to this and other sites on the internet.

3. Third-Party Links

Our site contains links to other sites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of such other sites.

4. Contact
