Frequently Asked Questions

How do I use this tool?

Simply enter your list of participants in the box on the left. Each name must be on a new line. Click the wheel or the spin button to select a random winner.

Is it truly random?

Yes. TeamSpin PRO uses the browser's high-entropy Math.random() function combined with physics-based deceleration to ensure a non-deterministic outcome.

Can I use this for giveaways?

Absolutely! Many teachers use it for student selection, and HR managers use it for office raffles.

Privacy Policy

We do not store your name lists on our servers. All names you enter stay within your browser's local memory.
