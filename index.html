    Privacy Policy & Terms

    Your privacy is paramount at TeamSpin Pro. We have architected this tool to be a local-first application. This means that every name, list, or sensitive participant information you enter into the input box remains exclusively on your device. We do not transmit, store, or see your participant data. Randomization is performed using your browser's built-in Web Crypto API, ensuring high-entropy fairness without the need for server-side processing.

    We utilize Google AdSense to provide this tool free of charge. Google may use cookies to serve personalized advertisements based on your browsing history. You can manage your tracking preferences in your browser settings or via Google's Ad Settings portal. We also use basic analytics to monitor site performance, which collects anonymized data like browser version and visit duration. By using our service, you acknowledge that your data remains your own, processed locally on your hardware.
