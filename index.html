Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1: How do I add names to the wheel?
A: Enter one name per line in the sidebar textarea. You can also remove duplicates, shuffle, or sort names.

Q2: Can I spin the wheel using keyboard?
A: Yes, press Spacebar to spin the wheel in addition to clicking the canvas.

Q3: Is my data safe?
A: Absolutely. All names are processed locally on your device. Nothing is uploaded to our servers.

Q4: Can I export my list?
A: Yes, click the 'Export' button to save the names as a CSV file.

Q5: How do I share or save the winner?
A: Use the 'Share Winner' or 'Save Winner' buttons under the wheel after spinning.
