How to Use the Random Name Picker Wheel?

TeamSpin Pro is a professional online random name picker. To get started, type your list of names into the participant box. You will see the wheel update in real-time. Once your list is ready, click "Spin Now" to pick a winner fairly. This tool is perfect for teachers and giveaway organizers.

Features of TeamSpin Pro

Our wheel automatically identifies duplicate entries (case-insensitive) to ensure fairness. Additionally, the system ensures you cannot spin with only one name, maintaining the integrity of your random draw.
