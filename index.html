About TeamSpin Pro

TeamSpin Pro is a fast, 100% fair, and responsive random name picker wheel. Designed for teachers, giveaway hosts, and decision-makers, it works perfectly on all mobile devices, tablets, and laptops. Our wheel ensures transparency by processing all data locally in your browser.

Terms and Conditions

Usage is free. Results are generated using a mathematical random algorithm and are provided as-is. We do not store your participant names on any server. By using this tool, you agree to our fair use policy.

About Our Project

Our mission is to create the most accessible decision-making tool on the web. We prioritize user privacy and multi-language inclusivity. Currently supporting English, Hindi, and Nepali.
