Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ) - TeamSpin PRO

Here are answers to common questions that users may have when using TeamSpin PRO for schools, offices, sports events, parties, and general activities.

General Questions

What is TeamSpin PRO?

TeamSpin PRO is a digital random picker platform where you can spin a wheel to select names, tasks, or prizes. It works on any device and stores all data locally for privacy.

Do I need to sign up or create an account?

No. You can use TeamSpin PRO without registration. All data is processed locally on your device, and no personal data is collected externally.

Is my data safe?

Yes. All entries are stored temporarily on your device. Sharing via social media or email is optional. TeamSpin PRO does not send or store your data externally.

School & Classroom Usage

How can teachers use TeamSpin PRO in the classroom?

Teachers can use it to select students for answering questions, assign classroom tasks, or randomly pick project groups. All student names remain on the local device for privacy.

Is it safe for children?

Yes. It’s suitable for all ages. Teachers are advised to supervise children under 13. No personal data is collected externally.

Office & Corporate Usage

How can this tool help in offices?

TeamSpin PRO can be used to assign tasks randomly, select employees for presentations, or choose prizes during office events. It helps in ensuring fairness in task assignment.

Can it handle large lists of employees?

Yes. The platform can handle hundreds of names in a single wheel, making it suitable for small teams or large office events.

Sports & Events

How can this be used for sports events?

Use TeamSpin PRO to randomly assign players to teams, pick winners for raffles, or select participants for drills and activities. This ensures fairness and excitement.

Does it work for tournaments?

Yes. You can randomize matchups, assign teams, or determine the order of play using the wheel. All entries remain private unless you choose to share.

Party & Entertainment Usage

Can I use it for birthdays or family events?

Yes. TeamSpin PRO is perfect for games, prize draws, and selecting activities randomly. It adds fun and fairness to gatherings.

Technical & Support Questions

Which devices are supported?

TeamSpin PRO works on desktops, laptops, tablets, and smartphones. It’s fully responsive and adapts to any screen size.

Do I need an internet connection?

An internet connection is only needed to share results externally. The wheel itself and all randomization work offline on your device.

How do I provide feedback?

Visit our Feedback Page to send suggestions or report issues. Feedback is sent via your email client securely.

Legal & Privacy Questions

Is this GDPR compliant?

Yes. Since all data is processed locally and optional sharing is user-controlled, TeamSpin PRO complies with GDPR principles.

Does TeamSpin PRO collect personal data?

No. All names, tasks, or entries remain on your device. Only data you choose to share via external services leaves your device.
