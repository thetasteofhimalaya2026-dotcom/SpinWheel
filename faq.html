FAQ

How do I add names?

Type one name per line in the sidebar textarea. You can also import names via CSV.

How do I spin the wheel?

Click the SPIN button or press the spacebar. The winner is displayed in a popup with optional sound.

Can I customize privacy preferences?

Yes, you can manage cookies, ads, and analytics through the preferences modal.

How do I share or save results?

Use the share/save buttons below the wheel. All data is handled locally and securely.
