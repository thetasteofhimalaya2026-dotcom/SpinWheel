Privacy Policy for TeamSpin PRO

Effective Date: January 16, 2026

TeamSpin PRO (“we”, “our”, “us”) is committed to protecting the privacy of users accessing our Spin the Wheel platform. This Privacy Policy describes in detail how we collect, process, use, and protect information entered by our users. We prioritize transparency and user control in all aspects of our platform.

1. Information We Collect

TeamSpin PRO is designed to function with maximum privacy. All data entered by users, such as names or wheel entries, is processed entirely on your device. We do not collect or transmit personal information to any external servers unless you voluntarily share results via email or social media. Your privacy is fully under your control.

2. Cookies and Local Storage

Our platform does not track users with third-party cookies. Minimal local storage is used for session management, storing temporary wheel entries, and theme preferences to enhance usability. All stored data remains on your device and can be cleared at any time.

3. How We Use Your Data

All operations of Spin the Wheel, including random selection and result generation, occur locally on your device. Data usage includes:

  • Processing wheel spins and random selections.
  • Allowing users to save or share results voluntarily.
  • Storing temporary preferences such as selected themes, font sizes, or colors.
  • Ensuring smooth, responsive performance across all devices.

4. Sharing and Optional Features

Users may choose to share wheel results through email, social media, or other platforms. TeamSpin PRO does not share, sell, or transmit any personal data externally without explicit consent. All sharing features are entirely optional, and users retain full control over their data.

5. Privacy Preferences

TeamSpin PRO allows users to customize privacy settings, including:

  • Clearing temporary data at any time.
  • Opting out of optional sharing features.
  • Adjusting preferences for local session data, including themes and font size.

6. GDPR Compliance

TeamSpin PRO is compliant with GDPR principles. We minimize personal data collection and ensure that all user data remains under the user’s control. Your rights under GDPR include:

  • Accessing data stored locally.
  • Requesting deletion of entries from your device.
  • Withdrawing consent for any optional data sharing features.

7. Security Measures

Although all processing occurs locally, TeamSpin PRO encourages the use of secure devices and updated browsers to prevent unauthorized access. No external servers store user data, reducing potential vulnerabilities. Users are responsible for maintaining device security.

8. Third-Party Links and Services

Our platform may include links to third-party services for optional sharing. TeamSpin PRO is not responsible for the privacy practices of these external services. Users are advised to review the privacy policies of third-party platforms before sharing any content externally.

9. Children’s Privacy

TeamSpin PRO is suitable for all ages, but children under 13 should use the platform under parental supervision. No personal data is collected externally from minors, and local data processing ensures compliance with child privacy standards.

10. Data Retention

All user data is stored temporarily in your browser session. You may clear this data at any time. TeamSpin PRO does not retain user information on external servers or databases, ensuring complete control remains with the user.

11. User Rights

Users have full control over any information they enter into TeamSpin PRO:

  • Access and view entries stored on the device.
  • Modify or delete wheel entries at any time.
  • Opt out of all optional sharing features.
  • Request information about local processing methods.

12. Transparency and Communication

We maintain transparency about data handling and privacy practices. Any updates to this Privacy Policy will be published on this page. Users will be notified via in-platform notices when significant changes occur.

13. Use of the Platform

TeamSpin PRO is intended for personal, educational, and entertainment use. Users are responsible for their entries and agree not to use the platform to store or transmit harmful, offensive, or illegal content. Misuse may result in termination of access.

14. Legal Compliance

Users must comply with all applicable local and international laws while using TeamSpin PRO. This includes restrictions on content, data sharing, and prohibited actions outlined in our Terms & Conditions.

15. Updates to Privacy Policy

TeamSpin PRO may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in platform functionality or legal requirements. All updates will be posted here with a revised effective date. Continued use after updates constitutes acceptance of the updated Privacy Policy.

16. Contact Us

For questions or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us at support@teamspinpro.com. We are committed to resolving inquiries promptly and maintaining trust with our users.

17. Backlinks to Other Pages

For easy navigation and further information, visit:

  • Home / Spin the Wheel
  • Terms & Conditions

18. Summary

TeamSpin PRO prioritizes user privacy and local data control. All personal data is optional and processed locally, sharing features are entirely voluntary, and GDPR principles are respected. Users have full access, control, and rights over their data. By using TeamSpin PRO, you acknowledge and accept this Privacy Policy.
